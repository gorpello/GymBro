# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want GymBro to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@gorpello](https://github.com/gorpello), through
GitHub. Every report will be reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or
remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following
the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
