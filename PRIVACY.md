# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

GymBro is an offline gym log. It has no account, no ads and no analytics.

## What GymBro stores

Your workouts, routines, measurements, notes, photos and videos are saved **only on your
device**, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to us, because we don't run any server.

## What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send:

- **Sharing** a workout card or sticker hands an image to the app you pick.
- **Strava export** creates a `.fit` file that you send to Strava yourself.
- **Routine with AI** copies a text list that you paste wherever you like.
- **Export** creates files that you save or share yourself.

## Permissions

GymBro asks for the photo library when you add progress photos or stickers, and for
notifications for the rest timer. You can turn both off in Settings at any time.

## Deleting your data

Delete everything from Settings in the app, or delete the app.
